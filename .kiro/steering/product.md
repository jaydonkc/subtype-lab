# Product Steering: SubtypeLab

SubtypeLab is a narrow claim-auditing tool, not a broad bioinformatics agent. Its job is to help researchers test whether cancer subtype and biomarker claims survive realistic data stress.

Default product framing:

- "crash-test lab for subtype and biomarker claims"
- "research support and hypothesis generation"
- "robust, suspicious, or fragile verdicts backed by computed metrics"

Avoid broad claims about SNPs, full multi-omics, paper-to-pipeline automation, or clinical interpretation in v1.
